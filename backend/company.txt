COMPANY PROFILE:
Academic Attire Co. specializes in high-quality graduation regalia. We have been in business since 1998, serving over 500 universities globally. Our core values are sustainability, affordability, and academic excellence.

PRODUCT OFFERINGS:
1. Doctoral Gowns: Hand-stitched velvet panels with bell sleeves. Available in black and university-specific colors.
2. Bachelor's Caps: Standard mortarboard with adjustable elastic.
3. Eco-Friendly Collection: Made from 100% recycled plastic bottles (rPET). This line is currently 20% cheaper to produce.
